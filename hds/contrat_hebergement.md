Cold aisle containment
Cold aisle cladding consists of doors and slabs that separate the flow of hot
and cold air in a datacenter. This separation is a key factor in reducing energy
consumption and improving cooling efficiency.
This "cold corridor" features sliding doors with windows at each end of the
cold aisle. Composite and metal cover slabs let in light.
The installation of this system avoids a significant increase in the quantity of
flammable materials, improves the efficiency and performance of the air-conditioning
system, and increases the possible level of thermal density in the bays.
Energy and air conditioning

This ensures optimum operating conditions for the equipment housed there. The room is thermally

the cold corridor (+/- 2°C).
The power supply is rippled and backed up by a generator (approx. 48 hours autonomy on full load). In the

the generator to start up. Our offer is based on racks specially designed for high-density servers. For

Electrical emergency mechanisms

Equipped with a 2,500-liter tank, the server center will be able to operate without interruption for at least

Fire prevention, detection and control

Service continuity mechanisms
Ensures optimum service continuity through the following technologies:
- Server virtualization: A physical server represents a computing unit. In the event of a computing
unit failure, the virtual servers on this unit are automatically restarted on another unit within a very
short time.
- Storage virtualization: Data is physically stored on hard disks. In the event of failure, fault tolerance
ensures no interruption of service and data integrity.
- Network security: All the Datacenter's network equipment (firewalls, routers, switching matrices) is

possible loss of one of the physical links will not disrupt the service provided by AvaCloud. The

Regulations
Invoices will be issued at the intervals specified in Chapter 14 and will be paid within thirty (30) days of the
invoice date, by bank transfer. Other methods of payment are only authorized by justified exception. Duties
and taxes are invoiced in addition. By express agreement, and unless a deferment is requested in good time
and specifically granted by the Service Provider, failure to pay by the due date, whatever the method of
payment used and whatever the reason, will automatically and after prior formal notice result in :
• All outstanding amounts are due and payable.
• The increase of all sums remaining due following the settlement date shown on each invoice, by
the amount of late payment penalties pursuant to Law no. 2001-420 of May 15, 2001 on new
economic regulations, with a minimum of one hundred and fifteen (115) euros.
The interest rate for late payment penalties is four (4) times the current legal interest rate (French
Commercial Code, article L. 441-3, paragraph 4, and article L. 441-6, paragraph 3).
• Recharging of all bank service charges for non-payment of the invoices concerned.
• Recharging of all litigation management costs: registered mail, costs o f proceedings with the clerk
of the relevant Commercial Court, where applicable.
• Suspension of all current benefits.
